Before you build a resume, pitch yourself, apply for the opportunity, or walk into the room, you need to understand the brand you are already carrying.

Your personal brand is not about becoming someone else. It is about making sure the right people can clearly see the value, story, and strengths that already exist within you.

Your First Impression

People form opinions before you even speak.

Let's make sure the right one.

If someone looked you up today, what would they think you are known for?

What three words do you want people to associate with you professionally?
What three words do you think people currently associate with you?
Where is the gap between how you want to be seen and how you currently show up?
Remember:
Your brand isn't what you say it is.
It's what people experience.
  • Be intentional
  • Be consistent
  • Be authentic
  • Be unforgettable

Your Audience

Speak to the right room.

A strong brand knows who it is speaking to.

Who do you want your personal brand to attract?
Employers, mentors, collaborators, clients, community leaders, scholarship committees, creative partners...
What do you want those people to understand about you immediately?
What opportunities do you want your brand to help create?
Remember:
If you talk to everyone, you reach no one.
Choose your room.
  • Be specific
  • Be relevant
  • Be clear
  • Be found

Your Brand Statement

Put it all into one sentence.

Now let's put it together. Fill in the blanks below in your own words.

Write your personal brand statement.
I am a who through .
Example
I am a creative marketing strategist who helps brands and organizations create meaningful experiences through storytelling, strategy, and intentional event design.
Remember:
If you can't say it, they can't share it.
One sentence. Made to travel.
